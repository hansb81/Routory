# Day map / Timeline icon size / Inter-item distance

> CLAUDE.md에서 이동된 상세 내용. 이 영역을 작업하기 전에 먼저 읽을 것.

- **Day map** (`dayMap.js`): a collapsible Google map inside `.app-top` (so it stays under the header while the timeline scrolls), opened/closed with the chevron handle under it (`.day-map-handle`, remembered in localStorage `triplog.dayMap.open`). `app.js#renderTimeline` calls `dayMap.update({ trip, days })` after every rebuild.
  - Non-flight items with a place or coordinates get a per-Day number (1, 2, 3 in card order; numbering restarts each Day, and a place whose location isn't found keeps its number), shown in place of the card's icon (a colored circle, `.item-icon--num`, sized 31px — 70% of the plain-icon 44px, see **Timeline icon size** below) and as a pin with a per-Day color (`--route-1..6`).
  - **Day colors are warm, not primary colors** (orange/rose/mustard/brick/pink/olive, `--route-1..6` — see **Color policy: pastel** under Conventions for the validation method). Unlike the first cut of this palette, these were optimized (a small hill-climbing script over OKLCH L/C/H, not hand-picked) so that all 15 pairs among the 6 clear the dataviz skill's normal-vision floor at once (worst pair ΔE 18.6, vs. a 15 target) — i.e. even when several Days' colors are visible together in one timeline (short trips, or a long one scrolled far enough that non-adjacent Days sit close), no two are a "which one was that again" pair for a normal-sighted viewer. Colorblind (CVD) simulation is the one axis this doesn't clear for every pair — see that Conventions section for why that's structurally out of reach for 6 same-family warm hues, and why it's an accepted trade-off here (the number is always shown too, so no card is *only* distinguishable by color). Because each swatch's ideal number-text color depends on how light/dark it is, each slot also has a matching `--route-N-ink` (dark warm ink for the lighter slots, warm off-white for the darker ones) that `dayMap.js` reads into `routeInks` alongside `routeColors` and applies as `--pin-ink` next to `--pin-color` on both the card icon (`decorateCards()`) and the map pin (`Pin` overlay's `onAdd`) — `.item-icon--num`/`.day-pin-dot` read `color: var(--pin-ink, #ffffff)` instead of a hardcoded white.
  - **The map shows only the Day currently focused by scrolling**: its pins plus a dashed polyline (`--route-line`, a warm dark brown-gray, not neutral gray), fitted to that Day's pins; entering another Day redraws it.
  - Items without `lat/lng` are geocoded from `"<place>, <trip.destination>"`, cached in memory only; results farther than 400 km from the destination are discarded; at most 30 lookups per draw. Flights are skipped (airports have no coordinates).
  - Pins are `OverlayView` HTML elements (not `Marker`) so CSS can animate them; the focused pin gets `.is-active` (scaled up, pulse ring).
  - The focused card is the last numbered card whose top is above a reference line 24px under the sticky area (`pickActiveIndex`). Since the last cards could never reach that line, `layoutTail` adds bottom padding to `.timeline` (`--timeline-tail`) so every card can scroll up to it, and a jump over several cards inside one Day rolls the focus through the skipped ones (`moveFocus`). `--app-top-h` (set from a ResizeObserver) is the sticky area height, used by `scroll-margin-top` on cards.
  - The Google script is not requested until the map is drawn on the schedule route while open.
  - `haversineKm(a, b)` (straight-line km between two `{lat, lng}` points) moved to `util.js` so `app.js`'s inter-item distance labels (below) can share it instead of duplicating dayMap.js's own copy.


- **Timeline icon size**: `.item-icon`/`.item-icon--num` are 31px (was 44px, 70%), font-size 13px/12px; `.timeline-item`'s `grid-template-columns` and `.date-items::before`'s connecting-line `left` offset were resized to match (`31px` / `31.5px` — half the new icon width, plus the card's own left padding).


- **Inter-item distance** (`app.js#renderDateItems`/`itemDistanceKm`/`formatDistanceKm`, PRD-less small addition): between two consecutive cards in the same Day, a small `.item-distance` label (e.g. `"1.2km"`, `"350m"`) sits on the connecting line, straight-line (`haversineKm`) between the two items' own stored `lat`/`lng` — **no geocoding is triggered for this** (unlike the day map, which geocodes place-only items when drawn), so a pair only gets a distance when both items already have coordinates (typically: added via the map picker, not typed as free text) and neither is a flight; otherwise that gap shows nothing, per spec ("거리체크를 할 수 없다면 표시 x"). Styled in `--text-warm-muted` (see **Color policy: pastel**).
